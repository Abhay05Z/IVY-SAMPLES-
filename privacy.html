Legal & Member Protection

Privacy Policy & Studio Terms

1. Data Privacy and Information Handling

Ivy Studio (Dance & Fitness) collects member contact details (name, WhatsApp mobile number, email) solely to assign batch slots, coordinate class timings, send event schedules, and provide customized nutrition consultations.

We never sell, rent, or distribute member details to third-party telemarketers or external entities. Member data is handled with strict confidentiality.

2. Membership Terms & Batch Access

Memberships (PRO 3 Months, PRO+ 6 Months, and ELITE 12 Months) grant unlimited access to scheduled gym and fitness floor sessions during working hours (Monday to Saturday: 6:00 AM – 10:00 AM, 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM).

Personal training sessions included in the ELITE plan must be scheduled in advance with assigned fitness instructors.

3. Studio Etiquette & Safety

To maintain an energizing, respectful, and safe studio environment at Mahima's Trinity Mall, all members agree to:

  • Arrive 5 minutes before scheduled batch start times
  • Wear clean non-marking indoor training shoes or barefoot on yoga mats
  • Re-rack free weights, dumbbells, and stepper platforms after use
  • Follow coach guidance during ice bath cold plunge recovery
